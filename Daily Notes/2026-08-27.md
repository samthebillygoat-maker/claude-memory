## Caporales — Kitchen Tablet Recommendation

### Decision
- **Recommended: refurbished iPad (10th or 11th gen), 10.9"+, ~$250–300** as the kitchen order screen — but the *printer is the real order-receiving device*, not the tablet.
- Cheaper viable path: Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ (~$134–220 for 11"/64–128GB).

### Reasoning
- The Caporales ordering system is a web app (Next.js owner panel), so any modern browser works — the tablet is a display + alert device, not the POS.
- iPad advantages: Safari holds a background tab reliably, Guided Access locks staff to one screen, ~5 yrs of OS updates, louder speakers, resale value.
- Cheap Android risk: aggressive background-tab killing + Wi-Fi sleep = missed orders. Fine as a spare or on a budget, but lock down battery optimization.
- iPad 11th gen list price rose to ~$449 new (June 2026 memory-cost hike), so buy refurb/used rather than new.

### Redundancy plan (the actual failure mode)
1. Tablet speaker will lose to kitchen noise → pair a ~$25 Bluetooth speaker.
2. **LAN receipt printer with buzzer** (Epson TM-m30III ~$350–400, or Star mC-Print3) that auto-prints each ticket — removes the tablet as a single point of failure.
3. Third layer: SMS/email order alert to the owner's phone.

### Setup checklist
- Wall/counter mount with pass-through charging; keep it away from grill/fryer heat and steam (heat kills batteries, grease kills touchscreens).
- Silicone case + screen protector, wipe-down friendly.
- Guided Access (iPad) / kiosk mode (Android) locked to the order screen.
- Auto-Lock: Never; always plugged in; 80% charge limit if supported.
- 5 GHz Wi-Fi; add a mesh node if the kitchen is a dead spot.
- Buy a cheap second tablet as a spare (~$134 Tab A9+).
